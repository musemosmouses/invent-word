## The entry

> phontom — the jolt of panic when you cannot find your phone while holding it in your other hand.

96 characters, one line. *Phontom* (phone + phantom) names the panic of a lost phone that was never lost — it was in your other hand all along. Everyone has felt it; no English word named it until now.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `./out` when unset). Deterministic, no network, no dependencies.
